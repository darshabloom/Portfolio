Studdy

A tutoring platform designed around the actual workflows of tutors, parents and students.

The problem

Tutoring often gets split across bookings, messages, homework, files, lesson notes and payments. I wanted the product to feel coherent rather than like a collection of unrelated tools.

My role

I’m working across product thinking, UI/UX, frontend and full-stack development. I’m designing flows, testing how they make sense from different user roles, and building the product itself.

A UX example

A rebooking flow initially forced parents back through tutor discovery. That technically worked, but made little sense for someone who already had a tutor. I redesigned the flow around repeat behaviour instead.
